# Airport app-based pickups

- The Port Authority designates where app-based (FHV) pickups happen at JFK and LaGuardia. Drivers must follow the airport's current pickup and staging instructions.
- Waiting or circling on terminal frontage roads to catch a request is not allowed. Airport-bound repositioning messages must point drivers to the airport's designated waiting and staging guidance, never to terminal curbs.
- The airport access fee in trip records is `airport_fee` ($2.50 for pickups and drop-offs at LGA, EWR and JFK, per the TLC data dictionary).

## Implications for agents
- Airport surge events usually come from delayed arrival banks. Supply should be pre-positioned 20-40 minutes ahead of the forecast peak.
- Driver messages for airport events must say "follow airport staging instructions" and must not tell drivers to wait at the curb.
