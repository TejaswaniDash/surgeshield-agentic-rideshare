# Accessible (WAV) service

- NYC TLC requires High-Volume For-Hire Services to provide wheelchair-accessible vehicle (WAV) service. Response-time performance is part of their licensing obligations.
- WAV trips pay drivers a higher minimum per-mile rate (see the minimum pay standard).
- Trip records flag WAV requests (`wav_request_flag`) and whether a WAV was matched (`wav_match_flag`).

## Implications for agents (SurgeShield policy P-4)
- A disruption plan must not pull WAV vehicles out of zones that have open WAV requests.
- If a hotspot zone has WAV requests in the last hour, the plan must include a WAV-specific incentive. If it cannot, the Compliance agent flags it for human review.
- Driver messages must not exclude or deprioritize accessible-vehicle drivers.
