# NYC TLC minimum pay standard (High-Volume For-Hire Services)

## What it requires
NYC's Taxi and Limousine Commission (TLC) sets a minimum per-trip payment that High-Volume For-Hire Services (app-based platforms such as Uber and Lyft) must pay drivers. The minimum is computed from the trip's time and distance.

## Current rates (effective March 1, 2026)
- Non-WAV trips: $1.283 per mile plus $0.681 per minute.
- WAV (wheelchair-accessible vehicle) trips: $1.601 per mile plus $0.681 per minute.
- Out-of-town portions use higher per-mile rates published on the TLC page.

## Rule history relevant to agents
- The standard was first adopted in 2019. In June 2025 TLC amended it (about a 5% increase for a standard 30-minute, 7.5-mile trip) and adopted rules against app "lockouts" that stop drivers from logging on mid-shift.
- Future increases are set through rulemaking, so the agent must read rates from configuration and re-check this page.

## Implications for marketplace interventions
1. Incentives (bonuses, boosts, relocation payments) are paid on top of the minimum. They can never be used to offset or replace it.
2. A rider discount or fare cap must never reduce driver pay below the per-trip minimum.
3. Restricting which drivers can log on during a disruption ("lockouts") to raise utilization is a regulatory risk and is prohibited by this playbook.
