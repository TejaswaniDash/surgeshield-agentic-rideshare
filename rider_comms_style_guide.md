# Rider messages during disruptions

- Be transparent: give the estimated wait range, say when prices are higher than usual, and offer at least one alternative.
- Do not blame other riders or events by name in a way that could be defamatory. Neutral phrasing like "high demand near Madison Square Garden" is fine.
- Include the MTA Congestion Relief Zone charge in fare estimates when it applies.
- Meet accessibility needs: offer the WAV option clearly.
