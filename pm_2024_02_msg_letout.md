# Postmortem: MSG concert let-out (sample, synthetic)

**Summary.** Requests in zone 186 hit 5.1x baseline at 22:45. The median wait peaked at 18 minutes. Incentives went out at 22:50, which was reactive.

**What worked.** Relocation bonuses of $5 in zones 100 and 164 pulled about 30 drivers in within 20 minutes.

**What did not.** Drivers repositioned after the peak because the event end time came from the venue calendar and nobody watched it. Total spend was $3,100.

**Lesson.** Start pre-positioning 30 minutes before the published event end. Spillover zones (100, 164, 230) need smaller bonuses than the epicenter. Keep surge at 1.8x or below to protect rider trust; conversion dropped sharply above 2.0x.
