# Driver messages

- Push notification: 140 characters or fewer, then a details card.
- Lead with the opportunity (zone and time window), then the exact committed bonus, then the expiry.
- Neutral, respectful tone. No urgency language like "hurry", "race" or "now or never".
- Always include the safety line: "Drive safely - follow all traffic laws."
- Provide Spanish copy when the driver's language setting is Spanish.
- Never mention other drivers' earnings or rank drivers against each other.
